Getting started

On Welcome, choose I want to vent (Ventor) or I want to listen (Listener), then sign in with Apple, Google, or email.

  • Ventors: set nickname, gender, avatar, speaking languages, and interests, then explore Listeners and book a session.
  • Listeners: complete multi-step registration (photo, about you, languages, comfort areas, boundaries, availability, training). Your profile may stay under review until approved.

Tagline to remember: Speak freely. Stay private.

Managing sessions

  • Ventors can search/filter Listeners, favorite them, book scheduled or near-term sessions, or use instant match where available.
  • Before joining, confirm price, duration, voice/video mode, and optional add-ons.
  • Join from upcoming sessions when it is time; end the call from the in-call controls.
  • After a session, Ventors can rate and optionally tip or report. Listeners can leave feedback as prompted.
  • Cancel upcoming bookings from session details (refund rules appear in the app).

Earnings & payouts (Listeners)

  • Completed eligible sessions credit your listener wallet according to your tier/rate.
  • Track ledger entries, request payouts to a saved payout method, and watch for minimum thresholds or fees shown in-app.
  • Keep identity and payout details accurate to avoid delays.

Ventors: rewards and invite bonuses appear under Rewards — redeem offers toward sessions when eligible.

Account & profile

  • Update nickname, avatar, languages, interests/comfort topics, and notification or privacy toggles from Profile → Settings.
  • Change password (email accounts) or manage linked social login from the device OS / provider.
  • Delete account from settings when you want to leave; some financial records may be retained as described in the Privacy Policy.

Safety & boundaries

  • Listeners set comfort areas and hard boundaries during onboarding — Ventors should respect them.
  • You can leave a session at any time. Use in-app report tools for harassment or policy violations.
  • Venting is not emergency care. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call local emergency services.
  • Do not share highly sensitive credentials, financial OTPs, or illegal content in sessions.

Community guidelines

  • Be respectful, non-judgmental, and confidential.
  • No hate, harassment, sexual solicitation, or exploitation.
  • No impersonation of licensed clinicians unless authorized.
  • No recording or resharing private sessions.
  • Follow platform decisions on warnings, suspensions, and bans.

FAQs

Is Venting therapy?

No. It is peer emotional support. Seek licensed professionals for clinical needs.

Can one account be both Ventor and Listener?

Not by default. Choose your role on Welcome; mismatch with an existing account is blocked.

Why is my Listener profile under review?

We review identity and onboarding quality before paid listening. You will see status updates in the app.

Which languages are supported in the UI?

English and Arabic. Speaking languages for matching come from the catalog managed by the platform.

App & technical

  • Use the latest App Store / Play Store version for bug fixes.
  • Grant microphone (and camera for video) permissions for calls.
  • Stable internet improves call quality; switch networks if audio drops.
  • Still stuck? Email support@venting.app with your app version and device model.
