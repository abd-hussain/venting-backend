Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 August 2026 · How Venting handles your information

Promise: Venting is built around private conversations. We design features so you can speak freely and stay private — we do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

This Policy explains how the Venting mobile apps collect, use, store, and share information when you use Ventor or Listener features.

2. Information we collect

Account & profile

  • Email, auth provider identifiers (Google/Apple), password hashes (if email auth), role (Ventor/Listener).
  • Profile fields such as nickname, gender, avatar, languages, interests/comfort areas, boundaries, bio/quote, and Listener professional details where provided.
  • Listener identity documents and verification status when required for approval.

Usage & sessions

  • Booking details, session metadata (time, duration, status, optional mode), ratings, tips, reports, and device/diagnostics headers needed for security and support.
  • Call media is transmitted to enable the live session; we do not use session audio/video to train public AI models.

Payments & rewards

  • Transaction amounts, promo/reward redemptions, wallet ledger entries, payout methods, and payout history. Card data is processed by payment providers — we do not store full card numbers on Venting servers.

Device & technical

  • App version, OS, installation id, language preference, approximate network type, and crash/performance logs.

3. How we use information

  • Provide accounts, matching, sessions, ratings, rewards, earnings, training, and customer support.
  • Verify Listeners, prevent fraud/abuse, enforce Terms, and improve reliability.
  • Send transactional notifications (session updates, security alerts). Marketing messages only where permitted and controllable in settings.

4. Sharing

We share data only as needed with:

  • Infrastructure, analytics, push, and payment/payout processors under contract.
  • The other party in a session — limited profile info required to run the call (e.g., display name, rating summary).
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect safety.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention

We keep account and transaction records while your account is active and as required for legal, tax, dispute, and security purposes. Soft-deleted accounts may retain historical session/payment records with access restricted.

6. Security

We use industry-standard controls (encryption in transit, access controls, least privilege). No method is 100% secure; protect your device and login methods.

7. Your choices

  • Update profile and privacy/notification preferences in-app.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion via support@venting.app (subject to legal holds).
  • Control OS-level permissions (microphone, camera, notifications).

8. Children

Venting is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. International users

Your information may be processed in countries where we or our processors operate. We apply appropriate safeguards where required.

10. Changes & contact

We may update this Policy and will revise the effective date. Contact: support@venting.app
